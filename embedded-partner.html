Where I can help

Five positions I can hold. Most teams need two or three of them, and which ones depends on the hole in the team rather than the stage of the product.

01

Design lead, or your only designer

Either position. As lead I set the standard and direct other designers; as the only designer I hold the whole surface myself, from strategy through to shipped code. I have done both, and I will tell you which one your team actually needs.

You need this whenYou have no designer at all, or you have designers and nobody leading them.

02

Product designer on the roadmap

End-to-end design at your cadence: flows, interaction, interface, and usability testing where the stakes justify it. More directions where the decision is expensive, one where it is not.

You need this whenThe roadmap is moving faster than design can keep up with, and specs are running out mid-sprint.

03

Design system owner

Tokens authored in code, component decisions written down, and validation that catches drift in CI, so consistency stops depending on anyone remembering.

You need this whenYou have a system nobody owns, or three of them, and engineers are rebuilding their own.

04

The person who finds what is leaking

An audit of the surfaces you already ship: states, flows, inconsistencies, brought to product strategy rather than filed as tickets.

You need this whenThe backlog is full of symptoms and the underlying problem has never been named.

05

Prototyping and shipping

Working HTML, CSS and JS against your data, so the team argues about behaviour rather than images. Front-end work lands on your branch and is reviewed in your process.

You need this whenDesign keeps stopping at approval, and the last ten percent falls to whoever has capacity.

Questions

The things teams ask before embedding

How is this different from hiring a contractor?

I write and ship the front-end, so work doesn't stop at a handoff. And I bring product strategy — I'll argue about what's on the roadmap, not just how it looks.

We already have designers. Does that work?

Often better. I take the system and front-end half, or the lead position, so they can stay on product depth. I've led designers and I've supported them.

What happens when the engagement ends?

The system is the handover. Tokens, component docs and validation in CI mean the team keeps the standard without me — that's the whole point of building it that way.

Is AI writing our product?

No. AI-native means I use Claude Code and Claude Design to move faster on exploration and implementation. Every decision, review and shipped line is mine, and gated by the same checks as any engineer's.

Could this become a full-time role?

Yes. I'm open to senior and lead roles, and several embedded engagements are how that conversation starts. No fee for converting.

Which time zones do you cover?

Remote worldwide, working in English, Spanish and Portuguese. I set my hours to overlap with your team, and travel for onsites when it matters.
