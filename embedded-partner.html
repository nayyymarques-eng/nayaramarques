Where I can help

Five positions. Most teams need two or three, depending on the hole in the team, not the stage of the product.

  1. 01

    Design lead, or your only designer

    As lead I set the standard and direct designers; as the only designer I hold everything from strategy to shipped code. I have done both, and will tell you which you need.

    You need this whenYou have no designer, or designers nobody leads.

  2. 02

    Product designer on the roadmap

    End-to-end design at your cadence: flows, interaction, interface, and usability testing where the stakes justify it.

    You need this whenThe roadmap moves faster than design, and specs run out mid-sprint.

  3. 03

    Design system owner

    Tokens in code, component decisions written down, and validation that catches drift in CI.

    You need this whenYou have a system nobody owns, or three, and engineers rebuild their own.

  4. 04

    The person who finds what is leaking

    An audit of what you ship (states, flows, inconsistencies), taken to strategy, not filed as tickets.

    You need this whenThe backlog is full of symptoms and the underlying problem has never been named.

  5. 05

    Prototyping and shipping

    Working HTML, CSS and JS on your data, so the team argues about behaviour, not images. It lands on your branch, through your review.

    You need this whenDesign stops at approval, and the last ten percent falls to whoever has capacity.

Questions

What teams ask before embedding

How is this different from hiring a contractor?

I ship the front-end, so work doesn't stop at a handoff, and I argue about what's on the roadmap, not just how it looks.

We already have designers. Does that work?

Often better. I take the system and front-end, or the lead, so they can stay on product depth. I've led designers and supported them.

What happens when the engagement ends?

The system is the handover: tokens, component docs and validation in CI keep the standard without me.

Is AI writing our product?

No. Claude Code and Claude Design speed up exploration and implementation. Every decision, review and shipped line is mine, gated by the same checks as any engineer's.

Could this become a full-time role?

Yes. I'm open to senior and lead roles, and embedding is a good way to start that conversation.

Which time zones do you cover?

Remote worldwide, in English, Spanish and Portuguese, on hours that overlap yours. I travel for onsites when it matters.
