What gets built

Four layers, in this order

  1. Foundations

    Colour, type, space, radius and motion as tokens named for their job, so an agent picks the right one, not the nearest number. Contrast checked on every pair.

  2. Components and templates

    The parts your product uses, in code, with states, limits and usage rules. Templates for repeating screens, so AI starts from what exists.

  3. Drift review

    What AI invents is tagged and reviewed: it becomes a component with rules, or is replaced by one that exists. Drift is caught in the pull request, not in a redesign a year later.

  4. Docs an agent can read

    A docs site your team browses and a coding agent parses: every part, its contract, and what to use instead.
