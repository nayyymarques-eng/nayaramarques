How the loop ran. The content-clarity skill: read the rendered page, list the gaps with its checklist, rewrite only what the gaps name, then hand the page to a new reviewer (a fresh sub-agent that had not seen any notes or earlier lists) for a cold read. Three rewrite rounds per page at most, then one more cold read to check the result. From round 2 on, reviewers listed questions of fact separately, since rewording can't settle them; from round 3 on, they were also told that the case structure (a decision naming the problem it answers) is fixed by §7, so it is not repetition.
