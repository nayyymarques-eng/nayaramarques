4. Proposals for your verdict

P1 · A token for the line weight: --stroke-diagram: 1px

Job: the one line weight inside illustrations and figures (rules, outlines, connectors, dashed paths). Added to tokens/borders.css, used by the windows and by .elsewhere (its dashed outline and path were 1.5px). Icons keep their own stroke; the 2px accent marker bars are markers, not lines. Written as ILL-05, marked proposal. Recommend: accept, then ds-sync.

P2 · Skeleton bars: no radius exception

On this branch no 2px corners remain. A skeleton bar stands for a line of text, so it is flat (level 0) and square, like text. Recommend: no mockup exception; square bars, as written in ILL-05.

P3 · Product radius inside mockups

The composer frames draw the composer with its own 10px corners inside a figure (DEP-03 would say 6). It is the product, on the scale. Recommend: keep, as ILL-05 says: product UI in [data-mock] keeps its radius if it is on the scale.

P4 · The landing diagrams on phones

At 375px the four landing diagrams render their labels at 8.8px (their phone formula), under 9.5. Sizing them from the rendered width (tried, then reverted, since you asked to leave them) gives 9.5px everywhere, but "Product strategy" on Project engagement then crosses the top rule (left: now, right: 9.5px). The check allows 8.5px for .ld-art below 480px until you decide.

Recommend: 9.5px on phones, with the long vertical labels started lower, in round 2.

P5 · Windows from 900px up

The case-card windows now show from 900px (were 720px); below that the card stands alone. Recommend: accept. A phone variant (one column) could come later if you want the drawings on phones.
